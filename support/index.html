Support

Getting started

  1. On your computer, copy the game's whole folder (the folder with all of its files, not only the one with the .exe) to your iPhone or iPad: in the Files app, under On My iPhone › Pocket Games, or with Finder file sharing.
  2. In Pocket Games, open the Library, tap +, and choose the folder.
  3. Tap the game, then Play.

Tips

  • Many Windows games work. Games that need Direct3D 12 or Vulkan, or that use anti-cheat, aren't supported yet.
  • If a game closes right away, check that you imported its whole folder.
  • Controllers: Xbox, PlayStation and MFi controllers work, and an on-screen controller appears when none is connected.
  • When something goes wrong, Settings › Logs lets you share a log with us.

Pocket Games Plus

Pocket Games Plus removes all ads. It doesn't change which games run. Manage or cancel it in iOS Settings › your name › Subscriptions. To restore it on a new device: Settings › Pocket Games Plus › Restore Purchases.
